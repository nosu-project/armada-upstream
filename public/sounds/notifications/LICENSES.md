# Notification sound licenses

Armada includes the following notification sounds. The displayed names are
Armada's names for the bundled files.

## CC0 1.0

The following sounds were dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- **Signal** — “Notification” by pedr01
  ([source](https://freesound.org/people/pedr01/sounds/840060/))
- **Pulse** — “Notification Sound 2” by deadrobotmusic
  ([source](https://freesound.org/people/deadrobotmusic/sounds/750608/))
- **Spark** — “Notification Sound” by AnthonyRox
  ([source](https://freesound.org/people/AnthonyRox/sounds/740423/))
- **Low bell** — “Notification Bell Sound” by LegitCheese
  ([source](https://freesound.org/people/LegitCheese/sounds/571511/))
- **Bright chime** — “Job Done Sounds” by Jofae
  ([source](https://freesound.org/people/Jofae/sounds/380482/))

The bundled files are Freesound's MP3 previews and have not otherwise been
modified.
